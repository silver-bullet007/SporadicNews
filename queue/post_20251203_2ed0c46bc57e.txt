India’s Central Bank (RBI), guided by Governor Sanjay Malhotra, kept the benchmark repo rate unchanged at 5.5% in its October meeting—marking the fourth consecutive time the rate has been held 🚦. The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) noted that core inflation has cooled significantly, providing space for the central bank to maintain its current steady stance on borrowing costs 📉. This move stabilizes the financial environment, allowing the Indian economy to digest previous hikes.
